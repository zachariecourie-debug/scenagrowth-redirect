Questions

Straight
answers.

Which markets does Scena cover for market entry?

France and the United Arab Emirates (Dubai and Abu Dhabi), with support into the wider GCC for companies already established in the UAE.

How long does a market entry project take?

It depends on the category and the entry model. Research and strategy phases typically come first; commercial development and launch follow once the model is validated.

Do you work with both consumer and B2B companies?

Yes. Consumer brands usually need retail, distribution and communication work; B2B companies usually need business development, partnerships and local positioning.

Do you guarantee results or funding?

No. We build and execute a plan with you. We never guarantee sales, funding or investment.

  4. CPNP notification

    Comply

Questions

Straight
answers.

Why should a Gulf brand enter France?

France is a large, brand-driven perfume and beauty market and a gateway to Europe. It is also demanding on compliance, points of sale and press.

What do Gulf brands usually underestimate in France?

Retail calendars, margin structures, labelling requirements and how differently French consumers read brand codes.

Do you handle the whole journey?

Yes, with one team: diagnostic, brand and packaging adaptation, press and creators, points of sale and sales follow-up, through to launch and beyond. EU compliance is handled with a specialist partner.

Do we need a French company or an importer?

Not a company to start with. You need a Responsible Person established in the EU and an importer, both chosen with you and a compliance specialist. A French structure is decided when volumes justify it.

Do you hold stock?

No. Your brand keeps ownership of its stock, its price and its registrations; the stock sits with a logistics partner.

Can you work from Dubai?

Yes. We work between Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Paris, with the French execution handled locally.

01 — The problemPlans vs. buyers

A plan is precise.A buyer needs to feel it.

Architectural plans speak to professionals. Buyers, investors and sales teams need to see the light, the materials and the life of a place — often months or years before it is built.

Without that, an off-plan project is sold on imagination alone. SCENA turns the plan into something people can understand in a few seconds — and remember.

Questions

Straight
answers.

What is SCENA Real Estate?

SCENA Real Estate is the property visualization and marketing line of SCENA Growth. It turns supplied architectural plans into still visualizations, property films and sales content for developers, agencies, architects and investors.

How does AI real estate visualization work?

We analyse the plan, define an art direction, generate still visualizations with AI-assisted tools, check each frame against the plan, then animate approved frames and edit the film. Generation is one step; direction and quality control are the rest.

Are the visuals architecturally accurate?

SCENA creates creative visualizations based on supplied architectural documentation. These assets are intended for marketing and pre-sales presentation and should not be treated as technical construction documentation.

What do you need from us to start?

A floor plan (PDF, PNG or JPG) is enough to start. Elevations, material choices, existing renders or a 3D model help us stay closer to the project.

Is SCENA Residence a real project?

No. SCENA Residence is a fictional creative concept made to demonstrate the workflow. It is not a client project and not a property for sale.

Where do you work?

On projects in France and in the UAE, and on projects marketed between the two.

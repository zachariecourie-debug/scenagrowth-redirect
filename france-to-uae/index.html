Questions

Straight
answers.

Dubai or Abu Dhabi — where should a French company start?

It depends on your sector, your buyers and the ecosystem you need. We assess both before recommending an order.

Can Scena introduce us to the UAE ecosystem?

Selected high-potential companies may be introduced to relevant UAE ecosystem partners and programmes, subject to eligibility and selection processes. Scena does not guarantee funding or investment.

Do we need a local entity first?

Not always. Market assessment and first business development can start before a structure is chosen; we help you decide when and how.

How do you support communication in the UAE?

Local positioning, content, events and media presence adapted to Gulf decision-makers.

Questions

Straight
answers.

What is brand localisation?

Adapting a brand for a new market — language, visual codes, tone, packaging and messaging — while protecting what makes it recognisable.

Do you produce the creative work in-house?

Yes. Strategy, creative direction, content and production are handled by one team between both markets.

Do you run influencer campaigns in France and the UAE?

Yes. We select creators for fit and credibility in each market and manage the campaign end to end.

Can you handle French labelling requirements?

We adapt packaging and labelling for the French market and coordinate compliance checks with the relevant specialists.

From product to shelf in France: the distribution map

For a foreign brand, the French market can look like a maze of intermediaries. In reality there are a few clear routes, and choosing the right one early saves months.

Scena InsightsRetail · UAE → France

The importer

The importer buys your products, clears customs, takes legal responsibility for compliance in France and resells to retailers or distributors. It is often the first partner a non-EU brand needs. In exchange, the importer takes a margin and usually asks for exclusivity on a territory or channel.

The distributor

A distributor holds stock, has a sales team and an existing portfolio of retail or foodservice clients. Some importers are also distributors. A good distributor brings relationships you cannot build alone; a weak one will list you and let your product sleep in the warehouse. Ask for references, sales capacity and a joint business plan.

The commercial agent

An agent sells on your behalf against a commission, without buying the goods. It is a light way to open doors with buyers, but you still need a logistics and import solution behind it.

Wholesalers and cash & carry

Wholesalers and cash & carry networks (such as Metro or Promocash) serve restaurants, cafés, hotels and independent shops. This is a strong route for brands targeting foodservice ("CHR" in French) or traditional retail.

Direct listing with a retail group

Going directly to a national retail group is possible, but it requires French-compliant packaging, a logistics set-up, the capacity to follow negotiations and to fund in-store marketing. Most brands get there after a first phase through specialists.

Specialist and digital routes

Premium department stores, fine grocers, concept stores, organic chains, pharmacies (for cosmetics and wellness), e-commerce and marketplaces are often the best entry points for a new brand: smaller volumes, higher visibility, faster feedback.

How to choose

Start from your target consumer and where they shop, then work backwards: which channel reaches them, who serves that channel, and what margin structure keeps your price competitive on shelf.

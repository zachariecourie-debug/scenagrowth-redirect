Montaji files,
prepared properly.

Montaji is the Dubai Municipality system used to register consumer products such as cosmetics and personal care before they are sold. Scena prepares a complete and consistent file with you and accompanies its filing. The application is made in the name of the eligible company, and the decision belongs to the competent authority.

01 — What we help withFile preparation

Most delays startbefore submission.

Missing documents, data that does not match from one file to the next, labels that say something else: we organise the file so it is coherent before it is filed.

  • 01

    Scope the project

    Product categories, number of products and variants, who the applicant is, and which requirements are likely to apply.

  • 02

    Organise the documents

    Collect and structure the pieces requested for each product, with a clear checklist of what is missing.

  • 03

    Check consistency

    Product names, formulas and ingredient lists, technical data, labels and packaging checked against each other. Points to review on labelling (including Arabic) are flagged.

  • 04

    Prepare and file

    A file organised product by product, then support with the filing on Montaji in the name of the applicant, and follow-up of questions until the decision.

  • 05

    Coordinate the exchanges

    With your local contacts — UAE company or distributor acting as applicant, laboratories, translators or regulatory consultants — and follow-up of questions.

02 — Who does whatDelivery model

Clear roles,no grey zone.

Requirements and eligibility depend on the product category and on the applicant. We say upfront what Scena does and what it does not do.

  • SCENA directlyScoping, document collection and organisation, consistency review, file preparation, support with the filing and coordination of exchanges.
  • The applicantThe application is made in the name of the eligible company — generally a UAE-licensed company with the relevant activity — with Dubai Municipality.
  • SpecialistsLaboratory tests, regulatory opinions or translations, when required, by qualified providers.
  • Not provided by SCENAScena is not an authority, a certification body or an approved representative. No registration, outcome or timeline is guaranteed.

Questions

Straight
answers.

Is Scena an authority or a certification body?

No. Scena helps prepare and coordinate the file. Decisions belong to the competent authority.

Who submits the application?

It is filed in the name of the eligible company — generally the UAE company acting as applicant for the products. Scena prepares the file, accompanies the filing and follows up.

How long does registration take?

It depends on the authority, the category and the completeness of the file. We do not guarantee a registration or a timeline.

Which products are concerned?

It depends on the category. We start with a scoping of your range to identify what applies.

Products readyfor Dubai?

Tell us about your range and your UAE set-up. We will tell you what the file needs.

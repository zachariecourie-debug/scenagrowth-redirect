Questions

Straight
answers.

Where does Scena run business development?

In France (mainly Paris) and in the UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi), in both directions.

Do you replace an in-house sales team?

No. We open doors, structure relationships and build the first pipeline; you keep ownership of the commercial relationship.

Can you introduce us to UAE ecosystem programmes?

Selected high-potential companies may be introduced to relevant UAE ecosystem programmes, subject to eligibility and selection processes. Scena does not guarantee any outcome.

How is business development priced?

It depends on scope, market and duration. We agree the model with you before starting.

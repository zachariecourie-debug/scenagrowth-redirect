Questions

Straight
answers.

Which retail channels do you cover in France?

Specialised retail, department stores, concept stores, e-commerce and selected mass retail, depending on the category and positioning.

Do you cover UAE retail as well?

Yes. We work with retailers, distributors and e-commerce partners in Dubai and Abu Dhabi for French brands entering the UAE.

Do you handle logistics?

We identify and coordinate importers and logistics partners; the partners execute the operations.

Do you guarantee listings?

No. We prepare, present and negotiate with you. Listing decisions remain with the retailer.

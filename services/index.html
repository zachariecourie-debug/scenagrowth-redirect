Questions

Straight
answers.

Which markets does Scena cover for market entry?

France and the United Arab Emirates (Dubai and Abu Dhabi), with support into the wider GCC for companies already established in the UAE.

How long does a market entry project take?

It depends on the category and the entry model. Research and strategy phases typically come first; commercial development and launch follow once the model is validated.

What is Scena Conversations?

Scena Growth's podcast and video series featuring founders, investors, creators and builders working between France, the UAE and international markets.

Do you organise events in both markets?

We design and coordinate events in France and the UAE, with selected partners when needed — for example Majlis Rare for luxury activations in the UAE. Each event is scoped with you.

Are events linked to content?

They can be: when it is part of the brief, an event is filmed and turned into video, podcast and social content.

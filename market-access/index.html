Market entry strategy:
decide, then sell.

Before any local setup, decide: a two-week diagnostic, starting from the shelf and the end customer, then a 90-day launch to sell on the ground, from France to the UAE or from the UAE to France, without opening a subsidiary.

01 — The journeyDiagnostic · 90-day Launch · Representation

Three steps,one decision at each.

01 · Two weeks

Diagnostic

  • Objective: decide in two weeks whether to launch, adjust or wait, starting from the shelf and the end customer.
  • What you receive: the shelf price, from transfer price to retail price, channel by channel; positioning against the market references; the channels to open, and in what order; regulatory requirements, with specialist partners; the launch budget and timeline.
  • Decision: in writing: launch, adjust or wait, with the reasons.
02 · 90 days

90-day Launch

  • Objective: in ninety days, set up what is needed to sell on the ground, make the brand known and go after the first orders.
  • What is set up: launch, creators, content and press around the brand; a sales team recruited and trained, with a pitch and tracking tools; points of sale: a prioritised list, first meetings, first orders; logistics: a chosen logistician, the online store connected, deliveries organised.
  • Review: on day 90, sales by channel, points of sale opened and the proposed next step.
03 · Month after month

Sales representation

  • Objective: a team on the ground, month after month, that sells the brand and keeps it visible, with no subsidiary to open.
  • Day to day: store visits and order taking, sell-out and restock follow-up, staff training, in-store activations, ongoing communication.
  • Every month: a dashboard shared with you.

Pricing comes after our presentation, with a proposal built for your brand.

Questions

Straight
answers.

Do we need to open a company in Dubai or in France first?

No. We decide first with the diagnostic, then sell through partners on the ground: an official importer, a distributor or an agent. A local structure comes later, only if the numbers justify it. See selling in the UAE without a company.

How long does it take to decide?

Two weeks: the diagnostic ends with a written decision, launch, adjust or wait. The launch then runs for 90 days, with a review on day 90.

Can Scena be the official importer?

Scena Distribution is being set up. Scena can become a brand’s official importer without buying its stock: the brand keeps ownership of its stock, its price and its registrations, and the stock sits with a logistics partner.

What happens after the launch?

Sales representation: your team on the ground, month after month, selling the brand and keeping it visible, with no subsidiary to open.

Which markets does Scena cover for market entry?

France and the United Arab Emirates (Dubai and Abu Dhabi), with support into the wider GCC for companies already established in the UAE.

Which brands do you work with?

Premium perfume, beauty, food and lifestyle brands, SMEs and mid-sized companies without a team in the target market.

How much does it cost?

Pricing comes after our presentation, with a proposal built for your brand. 20 minutes to frame it?

Do you guarantee results or funding?

No. We build and execute a plan with you. We never guarantee sales, funding or investment.

Questions

Straight
answers.

Do we need to open a company in Dubai or in France first?

No. The point of Market Access is to test demand first, using existing e-commerce and commercial infrastructure. A local structure comes later — only if the market responds.

How long does a market test take?

A first test phase typically runs over a few weeks — often around 30 days — combining e-commerce, content, communication and acquisition. Then we read the results together.

How is Scena paid?

Market Access is performance-led: sales commission, business commission, revenue share or a hybrid of a light retainer and commission. The model depends on the product, margins, logistics and level of involvement.

Who is the importer, the seller and the merchant of record?

Scena runs the go-to-market and commercialisation. Depending on the product, the importer of record, VAT and customs, compliance, stock, returns and merchant of record are defined case by case with the brand and its partners. Scena does not import any brand by default.

What happens if the test works?

We build on it: pop-ups, distribution, partnerships, retail and, when it makes sense, a local setup.

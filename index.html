answers.

What does Scena Growth do?

Scena Growth helps companies grow between France and the UAE. We combine market entry strategy, brand and communication, business development, retail and distribution, content, podcasts and events in a single team.

Can Scena help a UAE company enter the French market?

Yes. For UAE brands we run French market research, positioning, brand and packaging localisation, communication and influencer campaigns, retail strategy, distribution and business development through to launch.

Can Scena help a French company expand into the UAE?

Yes. For French companies we assess the UAE opportunity, define a local strategy and positioning, develop partnerships and commercial relationships, and support communication, content and events in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the wider GCC.

Does Scena guarantee funding or investment?

No. Scena does not guarantee funding or investment. Selected high-potential companies may be introduced to relevant UAE ecosystem partners and programmes, subject to each programme's eligibility and selection processes.

What is Scena Conversations?

Scena Conversations is Scena Growth's podcast and video series featuring founders, investors, creators and builders working between France, the UAE and international markets.

What is SCENA Real Estate?

SCENA Real Estate is the property visualization and marketing line of SCENA Growth. It turns supplied architectural plans into still visualizations, property films and sales content for developers, agencies, architects and investors. The visuals are creative visualizations for marketing and pre-sales, not technical construction documentation.

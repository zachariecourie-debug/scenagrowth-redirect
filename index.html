Questions

answers.

Which brands do you work with?

Premium perfume, beauty, food and lifestyle brands, SMEs and mid-sized companies without a team in the target market: French brands aiming for Dubai and Abu Dhabi, Gulf houses aiming for Paris and France.

Do you hold stock?

No. Scena Distribution is being set up. Scena can become a brand’s official importer without buying its stock: the brand keeps ownership of its stock, its price and its registrations, and the stock sits with a logistics partner.

How much does it cost?

Pricing comes after our presentation, with a proposal built for your brand. 20 minutes to frame it?

Do you guarantee sales?

No. We commit to the work done, measured every month (contacts, meetings, orders, restocks), never to a volume or a listing.

Do you need a company in Dubai to sell there?

No. You start with an official importer or a distributor; a company is set up when sales justify it.

A written decision in two weeks: launch, adjust or wait, with the shelf price, channels, risks and launch plan. See a sample.

You do. The stock sits with a logistician, in your name, like your registrations. We do not buy your stock.
